## Input

Coming from (internal) clients (Google products):

`(client, src campus, dst campus) → bandwidth demand (time series with daily resolution)`

- **Intent:** how much they want
- **Commitment:** how much we've promised them

## Global Allocation Tool

Expensive MIP solver:

`(src campus, dst campus) → bandwidth allocation (discrete value per quarter)`

## Deconfliction

- Quarterly resolution → daily resolution
- Client-aggregated allocation → per-client allocation

## Need to Balance

- Network utilization
- **Fairness**
  - Max-min fairness
- **Prioritization**
  - Service classes
  - Business-critical traffic
- **Business rules**
  - Business continuity: "If we commit to a certain amount, we must give it for as long as it's needed"
  - "If we committed something, and then we took it away because capacity changed, we must prioritize it over allocating new intent"
