A friend had an idea for Isomux that's growing on me - what do you think?

When spawning a new agent in the office, in addition to a blank slate agent, I can offer a few pre-setup profiles:

- Money Planner
- Side Project Builder
- Health Navigator
- Life Coach
- Research Analyst
- Personal Site Builder
- City Guide
- Todo List Assistant
- Code Reviewer
- Relationship Advisor
- Job Search Coach

Each profile would spawn with a dedicated system prompt, which, among other things, nudges them toward building personalized software. And, as they accumulate memories and session history over time, they'd become the go-to agent for personalized expertise on a topic.
