I rewrote the Isomux landing page *again*.

feature list -> narrative thesis

The previous hero line was: "A meta-harness for multi-device, multi-user, multi-agent collaboration in a cute office."

Now: "Where agents act like coworkers, not throwaway sessions."

The features are still listed, but only insofar as they support the thesis (how they make agents feel more like coworkers).

I think it's better now regardless, but especially important as I try to appeal to non-technical users. Developers think in terms of features. Most people don't.
