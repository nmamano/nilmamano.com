Multiplayer agents are the gateway for onboarding non-technical people. I keep saying this.

Yesterday, I helped my dad move his personal site out of a paid service (Ionos) and onto Vercel (free).

Ionos kept sneakily raising the price, and it was up to $23/mo. They take advantage of how hard it is for non-technical people to move a website. (The discounts kept escalating from 40% to 50% to 75% as I navigated the cancellation).

An agent recreated the site as a Next.js app. But the best part is that the agent lives in my isomux office, and my dad - who would never touch something like claude code or codex - can talk to it from his desktop or phone browser.

That's how he's been making improvements to his site. It works a lot better for him than whatever WYSIWYG UX Ionos has; he's sent 50+ messages.

And if he has any issues, I can step in and steer the agent.
