My take on agents solving frontier math problems:

Agents think differently than humans, so *their* frontier is different.

Like in Project Hail Mary, each civilization made discoveries that the other one didn't.

The interesting part about agents solving the Jacobian conjecture, etc. is learning about what kind of problems are easy for them.

E.g., we learned that they are better than humans at finding counterexamples.

If I was in math research, my priority would be to understand the nature of their frontier; the low-hanging fruit is in problems that are in ours but not theirs.
