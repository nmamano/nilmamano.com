Today, I bring you a little math delight: the knight's tour with the smallest number of turns.* Never seen before.

---

The image comes from a new algorithm found by a team of 8 Opus 5.5 and Astra agents orchestrated in isomux. The algorithm produces tours with 8n - 14 turns for every even n >= 48.

This beats the previous best known result of about 9.25n.

The agent team also improved the previous known lower bound of about 6n to 8n - 28, completely closing the asymptotic gap and proving that the above tour is optimal.

The only remaining gap is the constant, between 14 and 28.
