Weekend sidequest: VariantGPT

A chatbot that highlights the lowest-confidence words, lets you see alternatives, and swap them in.

The colors come from OpenAI's token logprobs, the model's real probabilities.

An interesting caveat: low probability doesn't always mean "likely hallucination." It's more often a signal of stylistic freedom, like "vibrant" vs "beautiful."
