Exciting news: a YC-backed healthcare company is running an Isomux pilot *right now*! They are A/B testing Hermes and Isomux.

What did they see in Isomux?

My new blog post, "Isomux for Enterprise: Agents for Normies" (link in comment), lays out its unique angle.

TL;DR: Thanks to agents, engineers now iterate much faster, but we are not yet seeing the same gains for non-technical employees.

Isomux can help bridge that gap. The key is that it's multiplayer, so:

1. Engineers set up the agents and their environment
2. "Normies" (said with love) play with them (including the "app store" for just-in-time apps)
3. Engineers step in whenever normies hit a technical roadblock

This is why I've been building Isomux to be both a daily driver for engineers and accessible for normies.

If you want to accelerate non-technical employees at your company, this one is for you!
