An http://isomux.app customer is using the office in a way I didn't foresee: like a terrarium.

He never talks to the agents in his office. He drives all of them from his own external agent, over an API token.

What he likes about Isomux is that the orchestration between agents is easy to inspect. The external agent kicks off work in the office, and then he looks at the office to see who is working and who is messaging who. Like a terrarium.
