I remade my personal site to tell a clear story arc. It's literally structured as a book 😆
 
Chapter 1: DS&A. DONE
Chapter 2: Agentic tooling. WIP

The idea is that, if I pursue a role, it should be self-evident how it fits in the story (I only pursue roles that do).

I'm trying to signal that Chapter 1 is CLOSED, but that, hey, the first time I was passionate about something, I pushed it remarkably far, from coming to the US to co-authoring BCtCI; now I'm passionate about something else (with receipts), so if that's what you're hiring for, you can get that same drive.

PS. I noticed that every time I remake my site, I subconsciously de-emphasize goog more and more. It was a great job, but the other stuff only happened because I willed it into existence, so I identify more with it.
