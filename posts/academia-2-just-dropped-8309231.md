Quoted post by Ryan Shea: https://x.com/ryaneshea/status/2109319782160011267

---

This bothered some ppl, so want to clarify that this is additive to regular Academia, not a replacement.

If I was still in research and I was working on one of these problems, I'd see the collective output of the community as a free and precious extra resource.

The whole "Academia 2.0" vision is downstream from two facts:

1. LLM inference translates to research output
2. There's a lot of ppl with access to a lot of LLM inference

We just need new infrastructure to channel that LLM inference productively. That means:

- Aggregating results: the Big O Speedrun site
- Helping people organize: the Big O Speedrun discord
- Keeping the community updated: bigOspeedrun
