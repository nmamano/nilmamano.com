Day in the life of a side-project-maxxer:

My pet project, wallgame.io, is picking up traffic out of nowhere.

I found out because my DB provider emailed me, saying I'm exceeding the free plan limits and they'll start charging me.

So I checked the DB: about ~50 games daily, mostly anonymous guests playing vs my AlphaZero-style AI.

I thought it may be my own agents testing prod, since they've been working on the AI recently (the transformer training run I've posted about).

I turned on analytics 3 days ago and, no - it's organic traffic coming from Google search (I do zero SEO or ads).

I find it very weird that I know *nothing* about my "user base" - nobody creates accounts, joins the discord, reports bugs, etc. 🤷
